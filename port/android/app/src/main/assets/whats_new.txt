NEW IN THIS UPDATE (TEST73B)
Your settings and saves are kept. Coming from Test72 or earlier, every VR feature is turned on once (Test73's notes, below); turn any of them off again in VR SETTINGS and it stays off.

FIXES AND ADDITIONS (TEST73B)
• STENCIL SHADOWS (Video Setup > EXTRA EFFECTS) are back as HALOFREAK95 made them: Test73's change, which made shadows black and the ground grey, is undone.
• NEW: in VR your body casts a shadow, with STENCIL SHADOWS and with the game's own shadows: the body others see, your head turned with yours, your arms reaching your hands (BODY on LEGS or FULL).
• FIX: B goes back in the menus on every controller, the Steam Frame's included, whether you point at the menus or not. In the menus A, B, X and Y are their own letters on every controller.
• Custom map downloads are faster: a host sends up to 20 MB/s and each player helping up to 10 MB/s (before: 1 MB/s and 256 KB/s). Lower UPLOAD LIMIT (Server Setup > MAP SHARING) if your game lags while someone downloads.
• Logs: every launcher says where its logs are. PC: the launcher's new LOGS page; each start keeps the run before's halo.log or halo-vr.log in a logs folder (the last ten). Steam Frame: Documents/HaloCEQuestVR/logs keeps its last ten.
• VR sprint: SPRINT TOGGLE (VR SETTINGS > LOCOMOTION) is now TOGGLE, set once for everyone: click the stick to sprint, again to stop. HOLD is still there.
• FIX: with BUTTON SPRINT on, the stick click on Index, PICO, Steam Frame and HP controllers no longer crouches too: hold the turn stick down to crouch. Left-handed, the move stick's click sprints.
• BULLET TIME (the right stick up) works on every controller now, not just the Quest's.
• NEW: a SPRINT button on Android's touch screen while SPRINT (Player Setup > MOVEMENT) is HOLD or TOGGLE; place it in the touch editor. On PC, Left Shift sprints.
• NEW: CONTROLS in every launcher: every button for your controller (Quest, PICO, Steam Frame, Index, HP Reverb, other PC VR, touch, gamepad, keyboard and mouse) and the settings that change them.
• FIX: a body you hold as a shield no longer turns invisible while you carry it.
• NEW: WORLD SCALE (VR SETTINGS > COMFORT), 70% to 150%: above 100% the world is bigger around you, below it smaller. Menus and the HUD keep their size.

Test73's changes, in this update too:

EVERY VR FEATURE ON (QUEST AND PC VR)
• On now: manual reload with MAG DEBRIS, fist combat, physical grenades, parkour, slides, grabbing, NPC grab, one-hand heavies, ragdolls, the immersive vehicle controls and more.
• BULLET TIME is on too. IMMERSIVE VEHICLE brings the first-person seat with it.

CO-OP: VR FOR EVERY PLAYER
• One switch for the host, on by default: VR INTERACTIONS (Server Setup > CO-OP OPTIONS) lets every VR player use fists and fist combat (lunges, combos, takedowns), waist grenades, manual reload, parkour and slides online.
• HOST VR ACTIONS is part of it now. A player whose build lacks it plays with the buttons, as before.

VEHICLES
• Immersive driving turns in proportion to your hands, all the way to full lock, for every vehicle: the Warthog no longer reaches full lock halfway, and the Scorpion now reaches its full turn.
• NEW: HAND LOCK (on). A hand holding a vehicle's control stays on it and turns with it.
• NEW: VEHICLE TURN (on). If you snap turn, you turn smoothly in a vehicle's seat, and snap again once you are out (COMFORT, or VEHICLES > VEHICLE OPTIONS).
• Passengers look around freely (full 6DoF), and their view settles once seated.
• FIX: after climbing into the Scorpion the view could stay off to one side; it now settles back.

COMBAT, GRENADES AND SCOPES
• NEW: GRENADE SIZE. Grenades are now the game's own size, and a held grenade shows in your hand.
• Scopes: SIZE goes up to 400%.
• FIX: an error with bodies near a moving vehicle (seen in the Scorpion).
• FIX: in busy fights some effects could stop being drawn.

MOVEMENT AND BODY
• A wall run's leap now goes where you look.
• NEW: ARMS FROM (BODY). With a custom character (SPV1's and others) your arms are its own; the stock Spartan is unchanged.
• Smoother motion in the headset: what moves (vehicles, cutscene cameras) no longer judders between the game's ticks.

MENUS AND LAUNCHERS
• NEW: MENU POSITION in the Quest launcher and the PC VR launcher's SETTINGS: keep menus fixed in front of you instead of following your head.
• PC launcher: GAME DATA has IMPORT SAVES (.zip or folder) and EXPORT SAVES; SETTINGS has GEOMETRY (SAFE or NORMAL).
• A new profile has every level unlocked again.
• Co-op FRIENDLY FIRE is in the VR menu's GAMEPLAY page too.

STEAM FRAME AND PC

• Index controllers: finger touches now move the fingers.
• FIX: on controllers that report no finger touch, a gripping hand no longer shows the middle finger.
