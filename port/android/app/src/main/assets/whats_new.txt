NEW IN THIS UPDATE (TEST71): EVERY CAMPAIGN MOD IN ONE TAP
• CUSTOM CAMPAIGNS: every mod now installs the way SPV1 does: choose it, tap "Download and install". The launcher downloads it from where its authors published it and checks every file (exact size and SHA-256) before installing. Nothing of theirs comes with the app.
• Project Lumoria: "Download and install" fetches its four levels from halomaps.org (about 460 MB). Like SPV1 it still needs Custom Edition's bitmaps.map, sounds.map and loc.map.
• The Altheros Experience: still marked "Experimental — currently not working" (not yet confirmed to run). "Download and install" opens its moddb.com download in the launcher's own browser (if the page asks, tap its download button). The 1.4 GB archive downloads (it resumes if stopped), is checked, unpacked on the headset (about 5 GB free needed while it does) and deleted, and the mod installs.
• Importing stays as the fallback for every mod, SPV1 included: from a folder, a .zip, or now a .rar.
• Deleting a mod's files also removes a downloaded or half-downloaded archive.

WEAPONS (Quest)
• MANUAL RELOAD now works with the mods' own guns. SPV1 hides every weapon's name, which had left all its guns reloading by themselves, its stock-looking rifles included; now each gun is recognised by its own model: SPV1's SMG, pistol, sniper and rifles (magazine, then the handle or slide), its shotgun (shell by shell, then cock it), flamethrower (canister) and launchers (tubes). The rifle with an underbarrel shotgun: its magazine by hand, the shotgun still reloads itself. A mod's needler is shaken to reload. Project Lumoria's guns already worked (its plasma cannon has no magazine).

EARLIER (TEST70), ALSO IN THIS UPDATE
Your settings and saves are kept. One exception: GRAB ASSIST still at the old automatic 10 cm (with NPC GRAB or FIST COMBAT on) becomes the new 15 cm, once.

LAUNCHER AND CAMPAIGNS (Quest and Android)
• This What's New page: shown once after each update; reopen it any time with "What's new in this version".
• Status labels on every custom campaign: SPV1 and Project Lumoria "Supported"; The Altheros Experience and Halo 1 MCC maps "Experimental — currently not working" (still there to try; send a log if you do).

Every file is size- and SHA-256-checked; its own ui.map and PC DLLs are left out. Needs Custom Edition's bitmaps.map, sounds.map and loc.map, as SPV1 does. Its four levels play from New Game, map kind CUSTOM SINGLEPLAYER (start with lumoria_a); the original campaign stays as it is.

• The Altheros Experience: still being worked on. If a level stops, the launch log names the step.

CONTROLS (Quest)
• Remapping never moves your other buttons any more. On VR SETTINGS > BUTTONS, press A (or left/right) on a row to choose a button: it shows with a "?" and nothing changes until you press A again.
• A button another action already has asks first: CANCEL, SWAP, CLEAR (the other action gets none) or BOTH (one button does both). The line at the bottom says whose button it is and what A will do.
• Every action can be set to NONE.
• RESET CONTROLS is the first and last row of BUTTONS. To reset one action, choose the button the bottom line marks (DEFAULT).
• A * on a row marks a button that does nothing for now (Button Sprint has the stick click, the grenade is on the grip, the grip with physical weapons); a + marks a button two actions share.
• FIX: turning BUTTON SPRINT off now gives PUSH TO TALK back its left stick click too.
• NEW: MIRROR BUTTONS (CONTROLS, AUTO). Left-handed, the face buttons (jump, use/reload and the magazine release) are on the gun hand even with the sticks standard. Your bindings are never changed by left-hand mode. OFF: as before.

WEAPONS (Quest)
• Locked weapons can be holstered: grip at a holster to put the gun away (the hand is truly empty: climb and grab with both hands), grip there again to draw. Letting go elsewhere still never drops it.
• NEW: Needler with MANUAL RELOAD: shake it deliberately to reload (a strong pulse when it takes). Aiming, walking and punching do not set it off; the reload button still works.
• FIX: SHAKE COOLING now works. A brisk shake cools a hot plasma rifle or pistol 1.5x to 3x faster (a light tick per shake); tiny movements do nothing. It works while the gun is hot, not only once overheated.
• FIX: Rocket launcher. After firing both rockets, the new tubes are ready as soon as they are in (no extra push needed).
• Emptied guns that must be charged (Assault Rifle, Pistol, Sniper, Shotgun) tick your gun hand every 1.5 s until you rack them, so you know why they will not fire.
• Pistol and Assault Rifle magazines now sit in the hand (centred in your grip). The Sniper's is unchanged. (vr.reload_grip_fit false: the old place.)

DISPLAY (Quest)
• Left hand: the Rocket Launcher, Sniper Rifle, Shotgun and Pistol now read the right way round (lettering such as SPNKr and PUSH no longer mirrored). The Assault Rifle keeps its fixed ammo display; Covenant weapons are unchanged.
• Each first-person part is drawn facing the right way whatever its size, so guns turned back for the left hand are never inside out.

MOVEMENT (Quest, optional, solo)
• NEW: SLIDE (LOCOMOTION, off by default). Sprint, then crouch (the crouch button, or lower your head with CROUCH SLIDES) to slide with your run's momentum, slowing with friction, faster downhill, steerable a little. Stand up to stop; JUMP CANCELS turns it into a jump (and a wall run). Slide into enemies: a Grunt is knocked down, an Elite staggered, a Hunter barely moves and stops you; a little damage, never a kill. Settings: SLIDE DISTANCE, SLIDE FRICTION, SLIDE IMPACT, CROUCH SLIDES, JUMP CANCELS. Turning SLIDE on turns on BUTTON SPRINT if no sprint is on.
• NEW: BULLET TIME (GAMEPLAY, off by default). Hold the right stick up to slow the world (TIME SCALE 25% to 65%; BULLET TIME KEY: HOLD or TOGGLE; its button can be remapped on BUTTONS). Your head and hands stay at full speed. Solo games only; not in vehicles, menus or cutscenes.
• Climbing holds better high up trees and on thin trunks and branches.
• Wall jumps go where you look: from the wall you are running on, keeping your speed, always away from the wall. Wall running itself is unchanged.

FIST COMBAT AND GRABBING (Quest)
• NEW: HIT ASSIST (30 cm): a punch at an enemy right in front of you lands without having to touch its face through Halo's collision bubble. Not long-range: farther enemies are still reached by the lunge.
• NEW: LUNGE HOP: a lunge begins with a small Spartan leap (OFF, LOW, NORMAL, HIGH).
• GRAB ASSIST: NPC GRAB and FIST COMBAT now bring 15 cm, and the 25 cm maximum now really reaches (it was being undone after the grab was found).
• Menu: FIST COMBAT now holds REACH, HIT ASSIST, LUNGE, LUNGE HOP, ENEMY HEALTH and COUNTERS; a new FIST OPTIONS section holds HIT HIGHLIGHTS, DEFLECT SHOTS, FIST REQUIRED and AIM STEADYING.
• Unchanged: airborne lunges, difficulty, blocking and parrying, target switching, body-part grabs, light and heavy enemies.
