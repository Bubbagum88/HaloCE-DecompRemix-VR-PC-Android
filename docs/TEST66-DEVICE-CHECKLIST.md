# Test66 device checklist

Install the Quest APK over Test65 (it keeps your settings). A log and a short
video help most. Note which items you tried. Nothing below has been checked
on a headset yet.

1. Warthog with IMMERSIVE VEHICLE: drive over rough ground and turn hard;
   the view never drops into the dash; turn the wheel past half a circle
   while gripping: it keeps turning and never lets go or flips.
2. Ghost, Scorpion and Banshee: still as in Test65.
3. IMMERSION > VEHICLES lists the vehicle settings; RESET CONTROLS works.
4. LEFT-HAND MODE ON: the gun starts left at a level start and after dying;
   holsters and pouches are mirrored; swapping by hand still works.
5. Manual reload (AR, pistol, sniper): the magazine sits in Halo's hand pose
   and does not shake; it lines up with the well, haptics grow as it goes in,
   a pulse when seated; the charging handle/slide follows your hand on its
   track.
6. FIST COMBAT: ENEMY HEALTH 6X needs clearly more punches than 1X; punch an
   enemy on a ledge above you (airborne lunge); block an Elite's swing with a
   raised hand, parry with a swipe; Hunters are not knocked down from the
   front.
7. Grab an Elite by the armour with GRAB ASSIST 10 and 25 cm; carry a corpse
   for a minute: it never vanishes; throw it: it stays where it lands.
8. Silent Cartographer: wall run along the rock walls and curved walls.
9. Throw a crate and a Warthog (OBJECT THROW 2X): they fly where you threw.
10. Grenades: at the waist by default, smaller; set DOWN to 80 cm: no warping.
11. WEAPON PULL: point at a gun on the ground 1-3 m away, squeeze, flick back:
    it arcs to your hand; closed hand catches, open hand drops it.
12. Dynamic ragdolls: kill enemies on stairs and ledges (they tumble); grab a
    body, let go: it falls on; DEATH PHYSICS CLASSIC restores the old deaths.
13. Custom campaigns: switch to SPV1 and play; import and install The Altheros
    Experience and play A10 from the Campaign menu; "Play the original
    campaign" puts Halo back.
14. Online: host co-op with MAP SHARING on and a Custom Edition map; a player
    without it downloads it and joins. Vote to kick from the scoreboard.
15. Regression: VR SETTINGS opens, joins from the phone, voice (and the
    talking icon), holsters, parkour, Test65's fists and Warthog lock.
16. Flat Android: the same campaigns, the PHYSICS row, Server Setup's new
    sub-screens, the campaign pause menu's SETTINGS.

Test65's, Test64's and Test63's device checklists are still pending too.
