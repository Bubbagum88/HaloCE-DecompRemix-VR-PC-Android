# Test59 device checklist

Install the Quest APK over Test58 (it keeps your settings). Logs and a short
video of anything that misbehaves help most.

## Vehicles (IMMERSIVE VEHICLE on)

1. Warthog: your hands are the tracked hands with fingers. Turn the wheel
   with one hand: the other hand stays where you hold it. The wheel is in
   easy reach (COMFORT REACH). Drive a lap.
2. Warthog with VIRTUAL WHEEL hidden: the grab is still on the model's wheel.
3. Ghost: both grips take your hands on the first squeeze.
4. Scorpion: the drive stick is in front of your chest right after you get
   in. Push it forward to drive, pull back to reverse, tilt to turn (TANK
   DRIVE). The tank view inside is as before.
5. Banshee: the flight bar is in front of you; it rolls (and slides with
   BANSH SLIDE).
6. With IMMERSIVE VEHICLE off, vehicles drive exactly as before.

## Network

7. Launcher > Browse: the Network selector lists All compatible networks
   (selected), then 28, 27, 26 ... 11.
8. Join an OpenCE network 28 server; host a game and have an OpenCE player
   join. Voice chat as before.

## Manual reload

9. Pull a magazine out, bring it back to the well and push it in: it locks,
   the gun fires with the rounds it had.
10. Pull one out and let go right at the well: it goes back in.
11. With DROPPED MAGS on, drop it, pick it up off the floor, put it back.
12. Reload while walking: the magazine and your hand stay steady on the gun.
13. A magazine from a pouch still works, empty gun still needs the charge.

## Ragdolls (DEATH RAGDOLLS on)

14. Kill a Grunt and an Elite with an assault rifle burst: they fall back
    along your shots.
15. A grenade or rocket throws bodies away with lift.
16. DEATH FORCE CLASSIC feels like Test58; HUGE throws further.
17. Throw a living NPC: it gets up within 1 second.

## Regression

18. Weapon release/drop/holster, sprint, reticle, haptics, parkour and
    climbing, NPC grabs, co-op joins and the phone build as in Test58.
