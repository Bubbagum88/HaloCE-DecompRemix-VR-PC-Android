# Test57 device checks

Turn **CONTACT LOG** on (VR Settings → BODY) and keep the log from
Download/HaloCE after each session. Tell us which log and video go with each
result.

1. **Warthog** (IMMERSIVE VEHICLE on): the virtual wheel is in front of the
   Warthog's own. Grip the rim or a spoke with one hand, then both. Turn it:
   right turns right, and the mark at the top shows the turn. Let go with one
   hand and keep steering with the other. Log: `immersive warthog steering
   from the gripped controls`. If a squeeze misses: `not taken by hand ...
   cm away`.
2. **Hidden wheel:** set VIRTUAL WHEEL to HIDDEN and grip the Warthog's own
   wheel. Adjust HOG REACH / LEFT / HEIGHT / SIZE if needed.
3. **Scorpion:** grip the drive stick's knob and lean it left and right.
   The turret follows your head. If the seat view is inside the tank, try
   TANK UP / TANK FWD (VR Settings → VEHICLES) and tell us the values.
4. **Banshee:** roll the bar to turn, slide it sideways to turn, push to
   dive and pull to climb. Try BANSH SLIDE off.
5. **Ghost:** as in Test56, plus a soft tick crossing straight ahead and the
   bar lighting up as your hand comes close.
6. **Toggles:** CONTROL GLOW off (no highlight), STEER HAPTICS off (no
   turning or limit buzzes; grab and release pulses stay).
7. **Switching:** Ghost → Warthog → Scorpion → Banshee and out again. Each
   vehicle shows only its own controls, centred, and your hands are free
   after getting out.
8. **IMMERSIVE VEHICLE off:** every vehicle drives exactly as before, with
   no controls drawn.
9. **Regression:** ragdolls, grabbing and lifting NPCs, throws, climbing,
   wall running and jumping, manual reload and charging, holsters, voice,
   co-op host and client, a network-26 server, scopes, reticles, left-handed
   play, flat Android launch and touch controls.
