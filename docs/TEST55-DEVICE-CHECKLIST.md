# Test55 device checks

Turn **CONTACT LOG** on (VR Settings → BODY) and keep the log from
Download/HaloCE after each session.

1. **Shades and vehicles** (LIFT VEHICLES on; Truth and Reconciliation or 343
   Guilty Spark): grab and lift an empty Shade, a Ghost, a Warthog, a Banshee.
2. **Named things**: pick up weapons and equipment on Assault on the Control
   Room, and bodies on 343 Guilty Spark.
3. **NPCs**: grab a firing Elite with one hand (it's held back, you feel the
   weight), then two hands (lift). Grab a Marine. Throw a Grunt into a wall and
   an Elite at a Grunt (`VR NPC: throw ... speed_mps`, impact damage).
4. **Ragdolls**: kill NPCs (DEATH RAGDOLLS on): limp heads and hands, no
   sliding, pushed from the hit.
5. **Reload**: take a magazine (held like in the gun, fingers closed), insert
   it, catch the slide/handle with an early squeeze.
6. **Calibration**: IMMERSION → POUCH PLACES / HOLSTER PLACES: the blue balls
   show while the rows are on screen and move with the settings; left-handed
   mirrors the pouches.
7. **Wall running**: angled walls, easing off the stick, chaining after a wall
   jump; the wall jump still goes where you look.
8. **Vehicles** (IMMERSIVE VEHICLE on): Warthog wheel rim, Ghost handlebar,
   Scorpion sticks, Banshee yoke (push/pull). Your hands and fingers show, and
   the controls re-centre.
9. **Zoom**: with physical melee on, a fist doesn't zoom and the right stick
   click does. With physical melee off, the trigger zooms as before.
10. **Performance**: a long fight. Watch `[render-perf] ... draws`.
11. **Regression**: voice in a lobby and a match; join a Build 188 server;
    co-op host and client; sprint, holsters, scopes, reticles, remapped
    buttons, left-handed play; flat Android launch, join and touch controls.
