# Test73b device checklist

Automated checks cannot stand in for these. Test73's and earlier device checklists still apply
(TEST73-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md and before).

Install the VR or Android APK over Test73 (1.0.40), Test72 (1.0.39), Test70 (1.0.37) or Test69 without
uninstalling; saves and settings are kept. What's New appears once, its Test73b section first. Send the log
after each session.

## Stencil shadows (first, please)

- Video Setup > EXTRA EFFECTS > STENCIL SHADOWS on. On The Silent Cartographer's beach and in The Pillar of
  Autumn: the shadows darken the ground, which keeps its colour (Test73's were black on grey ground).
- Turn it off and on again: the level looks the same as with it off, but for the shadows.

## Your body's shadow (VR)

- BODY on LEGS (the default): stand where the sun casts characters' shadows and look down. You see your
  shadow; it turns with your head and moves with your hands. Tell us if it ever looks wrong or breaks
  immersion.
- With STENCIL SHADOWS off, the game's own softer shadow under you is your VR body's too.
- No stencil shadow of the gun in your hand, and none on your first-person arms and gun.
- In THIRD PERSON and in a vehicle's seat: as before.

## B in the menus (every controller; the Steam Frame first)

- In any menu press B with the pointer on the menu: it goes back once.
- Point away from the menu and press B: it goes back once (the log says "vr: menu back on B").
- Hold B and move the pointer onto or off the menu: still one back.
- In the profile list X deletes a profile; Y and A work by their letters (on the Frame all on the right
  controller).
- Index and Pico: the left controller's upper button still goes back.

## Map downloads

- Host a game on a Custom Edition map; a player who lacks it joins and downloads it: faster than before (the
  downloading player's log says every 10 seconds "map sharing: ... bytes a second"). If the game lags while
  it downloads, say so (UPLOAD LIMIT lowers it).
- A player who had chosen an UPLOAD LIMIT before keeps it.

## Logs

- PC launcher: LOGS (first page). It names halo.log, halo-vr.log, the logs folder and debug.txt; OPEN THE
  GAME'S FOLDER and OPEN THE LOGS FOLDER open them (the logs folder appears from the second start).
- Start the PC game twice: logs\ holds the first run's halo.log as halo_<date>_<time>.log.
- Steam Frame: Documents/HaloCEQuestVR/logs keeps the last ten runs' logs.

## Sprint (VR)

- Over Test73 with SPRINT TOGGLE at HOLD: the first start says in the log "vr: SPRINT TOGGLE at HOLD, its old
  default, goes to TOGGLE, once"; VR SETTINGS > LOCOMOTION shows TOGGLE. Click the move stick: you sprint until
  you click it again (a vehicle's seat stops it). Set HOLD, restart: HOLD stays.
- Index, Pico, Steam Frame or HP, BUTTON SPRINT on: the move stick's click sprints and no longer crouches; hold the
  turn stick down to crouch. In a vehicle the click is still the horn.
- Left-handed (VR SETTINGS: LEFT-HAND MODE ON, MIRROR CONTROLS AUTO): the move stick's click (now on the right
  controller) sprints.
- BULLET TIME on: push the right stick up on any controller (not only the Quest's).

## Sprint (flat)

- Android, Player Setup > MOVEMENT > SPRINT HOLD or TOGGLE: a SPRINT circle appears on the touch screen (none over
  the menus, none with SPRINT OFF or AUTO). Hold it to sprint (HOLD); tap it to start and again to stop (TOGGLE).
- The touch editor moves and resizes it; a preset saved before this update still imports.
- PC: with SPRINT HOLD or TOGGLE, Left Shift sprints; the launcher's SETTINGS says so.

## CONTROLS

- Quest launcher: "Controls: every button, for your controller" (beside the Field guide, and on the game-data
  screen). Your headset's page opens first; "(change)" lists the others. Read your controller's page: anything
  that does not match the game, tell us.
- PC launcher: CONTROLS on the first page; the controller buttons switch the page; the wheel scrolls it.

## Held bodies

- Grab a dead enemy with both hands and hold it up as a shield; walk, turn and look around it: it stays drawn
  (the log says "VR NPC: held corpse ... its root brought to the body in the hands" as it did). Enemies' shots
  hit it.

## WORLD SCALE

- VR SETTINGS > COMFORT > WORLD SCALE: at 120% the world looks a fifth bigger around you, at 80% smaller; the
  change shows at once (the log says "vr: world scale 120% ..."). Menus and the HUD keep their size. Back at
  100% it is as before.

## Reports we need more on

  screen, what you changed, and halo.log from the launcher's LOGS page.
- 343 Guilty Spark, "after cutscene that introduces the flood my camera stuck": the log of that run, solo or
  co-op, the CUTSCENES setting, and whether the view came back.
- "hud ... attached to gun and tiny, it randomly disappears": is VR SETTINGS > HUD > WRIST HUD on? A screenshot or
  a short video and the log.
- The Maw, "a large count down during the cutscene, still on my screen": the bridge's own screen keeps the stopped
  self-destruct count after the cutscene, as on the Xbox. Tell us if a count follows your view away from the
  bridge, or keeps counting after Guilty Spark stops it (a screenshot or video).
