# Test70 device checklist

Automated checks cannot substitute for these. Test69's, Test68's and Test67's
device checklists still apply (TEST69-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md and before).

Install over Test69 without uninstalling; saves and settings kept (GRAB
ASSIST at exactly 10 cm with NPC GRAB or FIST COMBAT on becomes 15 cm, once).

Launcher
- What's New appears once after installing, not on the next launch; the
  launcher button reopens it; its GitHub button opens the browser.
- CUSTOM CAMPAIGNS buttons show their status; MCC section shows its status.
- Project Lumoria: Prepare, import the .zip, Install; lumoria_a starts from
  New Game, CUSTOM SINGLEPLAYER and plays into lumoria_b; the original
  campaign still loads. Restore / Delete behave.
- MCC: an imported MCC map loads (or send the log); The Altheros Experience
  b40 (send the log if it stops).

Controls
- BUTTONS: change USE / RELOAD to A: the hints line says A IS JUMP'S; A
  asks; each of CANCEL, SWAP, CLEAR, BOTH does what it says; NONE works;
  RESET CONTROLS (top and bottom). Rows marked * with Button Sprint on, + when shared; every row fits.
- Left-hand mode with MIRROR CONTROLS OFF: B (use/reload, magazine release)
  is on the left controller; MIRROR BUTTONS OFF puts it back on the right.
- Other settings rows still change with A as before.

Weapons
- LOCKED: grip at a holster stores the gun; climb with both hands; grip
  there to draw. Grip release elsewhere never drops it.
- Needler with MANUAL RELOAD: a deliberate shake reloads; aiming, walking and
  punching do not; the reload button still reloads.
- SHAKE COOLING on: shaking a hot plasma rifle/pistol cools it visibly
  faster; tiny movement does not.
- Rocket launcher emptied: new tubes in, it fires at once.
- AR, pistol, sniper emptied: charge to fire; the gun hand ticks until done.
- Left hand: rocket launcher, sniper, shotgun, pistol lettering correct and
  the guns not inside-out; AR display as before; Covenant guns as before.
- Pistol and AR magazines in the hand; Sniper's as before.

Movement
- SLIDE on: sprint + crouch slides; stand ends it; jump cancels into a jump
  (and a wall run); slide into a Grunt (knocked down) and a Hunter (stops);
  CROUCH SLIDES with the head. SLIDE off: crouching while running as before.
- BULLET TIME on: right stick up slows time, released returns; TOGGLE; no
  effect online, in vehicles, menus, cutscenes.
- Climb a tree high up its trunk; wall jumps go where you look (log lines
  "VR parkour: wall jump look=... out=..." if one surprises you).

Fist combat
- A close punch lands without touching the face; a lunge leaps a little;
  GRAB ASSIST at 25 cm grabs a body that far away; light/heavy grabs as
  before.

Report build, device/OS, map, settings, actions and the launch log.
