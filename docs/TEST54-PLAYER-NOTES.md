# Test54 player notes

Private test build 1.0.21-test54 (Android code 66), after Test53. Installs
over Test53 and v1.0.19 and keeps your data. Read the bundled guide's Test54
section ([TEST54-FIELD-GUIDE.md](TEST54-FIELD-GUIDE.md)) and test with
[TEST54-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md](TEST54-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md).

- Manual reload works end to end: B drops the magazine, fresh ones are at
  your free-side hip and shoulder, push one home, and charge only an empty gun.
- Marines, Grunts, Jackals, Elites and bodies can be grabbed by touching any
  part of them; NPC HOLD, GRAB ASSIST and THROW tune the feel.
- Thrown bodies stay where they land; DEATH RAGDOLLS (new) makes killed NPCs
  fall physically.
- Climbing grips rock faces, slopes, overhangs and ledge tops; hands pushed
  into walls hold on.
- Open hands grab, fists and guns strike; grab/climb features turn on FIST
  REQUIRED.
- Smoother fingers, hands that stay with the controller, and the assault
  rifle's handle no longer slides while walking.
- IMMERSION (features) and IMMERSION+ (technical options); features turn on
  what they need.
- Voice, wall run, wall jump, multiplayer and everything else are unchanged.
  Online notes: [TEST54-NETWORK.md](TEST54-NETWORK.md).
