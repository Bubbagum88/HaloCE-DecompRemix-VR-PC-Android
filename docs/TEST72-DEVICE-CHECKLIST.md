# Test72 device checklist

Automated checks cannot substitute for these. Test71's and earlier device
checklists still apply (TEST71-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md and before).

Install the VR or Android APK over Test70 (1.0.37) or Test69 without
uninstalling; saves and settings kept. What's New appears once, its Test72
section first.

## The left eye (first, please)

- Play SPV1 (or a busy multiplayer match) as usual until the left eye shows
  models stretched or garbled, or for ten minutes. Note whether it happened.
- Then VR SETTINGS > DISPLAY > EYE ORDER: RIGHT FIRST, and play the same way.
- Tell us: did it happen at all now? If it did, in which eye? Send both logs:
  `[vr-eyes]` lines compare what each eye draws, and "the eyes drew
  different amounts" marks a lead.
- Set EYE ORDER back to LEFT FIRST afterwards unless RIGHT FIRST cured it.

## Fist combat (Quest, Normal and Legendary)

- Punch toward an enemy while looking slightly away; uppercuts; hooks.
- A lunge from 10 m leaps to the enemy; from a ledge it dives.
- Grab an Elite with one hand and punch it with the other: every punch
  lands.
- Punch a Shade (you must not die) and its gunner.
- COMBO DISPLAY on: the counter climbs, and it resets after 3 s idle.
- Send the log: refused punches say why.

## Vehicles, menus and controls

- Warthog passenger seat and the Scorpion's riders: aim and shoot by hand;
  the reticle sits on the gun's line. Left-handed too.
- MENU POSITION WORLD: open the pause menu, lean toward it, walk, click a
  stick (it comes back in front). FOLLOW: turn away and wait.
- BULLET TIME on R UP: switch it on, get into a Warthog, it turns off and on
  there; out again.
- LEFT-HAND MODE: BUTTONS rows name the buttons you press; Back is the right
  B in every menu.
- SCOPES: each weapon's page is titled and readable.

## Other headsets

- Pico 4 / Neo 3 / 4 Ultra and Steam Frame: the game starts, the buttons do
  what the BUTTONS page says, and the page names them as the controllers do.

## Windows (PC)

- The launcher opens before the game: PLAY, GAME DATA > IMPORT FROM A DISC
  IMAGE with your Xbox image, VERIFY, PLAY.
- PLAY IN VR with SteamVR, Quest Link, Virtual Desktop or Pico Connect: the
  game in the headset, both controllers.
- Video Setup > STEREO 3D on a 3D TV or with red-cyan glasses.
- Controls Setup: give a key that another action has: the notice names it;
  Delete, Backspace, DEFAULTS.
- Player profile > MOVEMENT: sprint, slide, wall run, wall jump.
