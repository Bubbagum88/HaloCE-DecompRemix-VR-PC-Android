# Test65 device checklist

Install the Quest APK over Test64 (it keeps your settings). A log and a short
video help most. Note which items you tried.

1. FIST COMBAT on (IMMERSION > FIST COMBAT). Look at an enemy 3-6 m away,
   make a fist and punch: you dash to it and the hit lands on arrival.
2. Keep punching (left, right): white lights over the enemy count your combo;
   at 8 they turn gold; a hard punch then is a takedown (knocked down).
3. Let an Elite swing at you: a blue light pulses over it and both hands tap;
   punch toward it: its blow does not land and it is knocked down.
4. Get hit mid-combo: the lights reset. Enemies take more punches (ENEMY
   HEALTH 3X); try 1X and 6X.
5. Warthog with IMMERSIVE VEHICLE: no purple bar; drive over rough ground and
   turn hard: the wheel stays on the dash under your hands, no snapping.
   Try COCKPIT LOCK off to compare; GRIP GUIDE on shows grip pads.
6. Rack the pistol's slide with the other hand: the gun stays in its hand.
   Palms together and grip still passes it (HAND SWAP 10 cm).
7. Pick up and throw a Shade turret: you do not die.
8. Pick up a friendly Marine and carry it around: it lives. Throw an enemy
   corpse far: it stays where it lands.
9. Grab a living Grunt by the arm: it stays standing (held); lift it: it goes
   limp in your hand. Push an NPC slowly with an open hand: it moves and its
   body reacts.
10. Point at a rifle on the ground within a metre: it glows; grip: it flies
    to your hand and is equipped.
11. Grab crates and turrets: they should take more easily.
12. Regression: VR SETTINGS opens, multiplayer join from the phone, voice,
    manual reload, holsters, grenades, parkour, the Ghost/Scorpion/Banshee
    controls.
