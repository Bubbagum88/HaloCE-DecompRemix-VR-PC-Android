# Test71 device checklist

Automated checks cannot substitute for these. Test70's and earlier device
checklists still apply (TEST70-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md and before).

Install over Test70 (1.0.37) without uninstalling; saves and settings kept.

- What's New appears once, its Test71 section first.
- Project Lumoria with none of its files: *Download and install* downloads
  four levels (progress shown), installs; lumoria_a plays from New Game,
  CUSTOM SINGLEPLAYER. Interrupt it once (leave the launcher): tapping again
  resumes from the level it stopped at.
- The Altheros Experience: *Download and install* opens moddb.com in the
  launcher's browser; the download starts by itself or after its download
  button; progress counts to 1409 MB; then "Checking", "Unpacking" for each
  file, and the install. Note the free space before and after (the archive
  is deleted). If moddb refuses (the status line says "the host answered
  403"), report it and import the .rar instead.
- *Import ... from a .zip or .rar* with the owner's Altheros .rar: unpacks
  and reports 13 files.
- SPV1: unchanged, *Download and install*.
- SPV1 with MANUAL RELOAD: the assault rifle, pistol, SMG (a30), sniper
  (a50) reload by hand (magazine out, new one in, handle or slide); the
  shotgun (a30) shell by shell, then cock it; the rifle with the underbarrel
  shotgun: its magazine by hand, its shotgun by itself; the flamethrower,
  launchers; the needler shaken. The stock campaign's guns as in Test70.
- Delete for each mod frees its files, including a half-downloaded archive.
