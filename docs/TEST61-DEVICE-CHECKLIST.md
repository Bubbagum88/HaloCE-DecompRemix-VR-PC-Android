# Test61 device checklist

Install the Quest APK over Test60 (it keeps your settings). Logs and a short
video of anything that misbehaves help most.

## Immersive vehicles (IMMERSIVE VEHICLE on, HORIZON as you like it)

1. Drive the Warthog over rough ground (Blood Gulch's hills): your hands stay
   on the wheel, and bumps and rolls don't steer it by themselves.
2. Turn the wheel hard both ways: your view doesn't sway with it.
3. The wheel stays in one place for the whole drive (never jumps to your
   chest).
4. Ghost, Scorpion and Banshee still steer as in Test60.
5. In a fresh profile (or with VIEW on THIRD PERSON), turn IMMERSIVE VEHICLE
   on: VIEW goes to FIRST PERSON. Choose THIRD PERSON: IMMERSIVE VEHICLE goes
   off.

## Impacts and ragdolls

6. Slam a marine onto the ground and throw one into a wall: it is hurt, and
   the log has `VR impact:` lines.
7. Throw a Grunt into an Elite: both are hurt.
8. A grenade or rocket into a group (in a long fight with many bodies about):
   every enemy ragdolls. If one doesn't, the log says `death ragdoll
   skipped` and why.
9. A dead Elite or Hunter lifts with one hand; a living one still needs two.
10. NPC GRAB on: CUSTOM NPC RIGS comes on; WORLD GRAB on: CUSTOM SCENERY.

## Reload

11. Open a menu, close it with the button you reload with still held: no
    reload starts, and the gun keeps firing.

## Regression

12. Ragdolls, DEATH FORCE, NPCs up within 1 second, manual reload, the
    tank's drive stick, network 28 joins, voice chat and the phone build as
    in Test60. With many bodies at once, note any slowdown (24 ragdolls can
    cost more than 8 did).
