# Test63 device checklist

Install over Test62 (settings kept), or fresh. Logs and a short video of
anything that misbehaves help most. Every item is device-pending: automated
checks passed on the host, but not one has been tried in the headset.

## Reticle and buttons

1. With BUTTON SPRINT off, the left stick's click toggles the reticle.
2. Hide the reticle, then turn BUTTON SPRINT on: the reticle comes back,
   the click sprints, and it never hides the reticle again.
3. Turn BUTTON SPRINT off: the click toggles the reticle again.
4. Stand at the Warthog with MANUAL RELOAD on and press X: you get in, and
   your magazine stays in.

## Chest grenades (PHYS GRENADES on)

5. Look around: the frag (left) and plasma (right) stay on your chest. They
   follow only when you turn your body (past ~35 degrees).
6. They're the game's grenade models, in the slots and in your hand.
7. Reach for a slot: a light tick. Squeeze: you hold it. Throw: it flies with
   your arm (THROW POWER 1.5X).

## Fist assist (FIST COMBAT on)

8. Look at a Grunt or Elite 1.5–2.5 m away and punch: a short step brings
   you into range and the punch lands.
9. Chain punches on the same enemy; it isn't frozen.
10. Punch, then grab it with Grip: the assist lets go, the grab takes over.
11. FIST COMBAT off: physical melee as in Test62.

## World and NPCs

12. b30 beach: grab and lift a purple Covenant crate.
13. ONE-HAND HEAVIES on: one hand lifts a living Elite. Off: one hand holds
    it in place, the second lifts.

## Vehicles (IMMERSIVE VEHICLE on)

14. Warthog: one wheel (the model's own) in your hands, turning as you steer.
    VIRTUAL WHEEL hidden: as Test62.
15. Ghost, Scorpion, Banshee: the new controls look right and work as before.
16. Seated, open VEHICLES and move CTRL REACH / HOG REACH: the blue preview
    moves with the value, and your hands take the controls where it shows.

## Settings

17. VR SETTINGS > IMMERSION opens a list of topics; each opens its section,
    and B returns to the list. VEHICLES opens a list of pages.
18. New defaults on a fresh install: WALL SPEED 1.5X, WALL LEAP 1.5X, THROW
    POWER 1.5X, FIST REACH 2.5 M.

## Regression

19. Impacts, reload and its haptics, magazines, charging, grabbing, throw and
    lift, ragdolls, climbing, wall running, sprint, holsters, aiming,
    scopes, full-body IK, left-handed play, co-op, voice and the phone build
    as in Test62.
