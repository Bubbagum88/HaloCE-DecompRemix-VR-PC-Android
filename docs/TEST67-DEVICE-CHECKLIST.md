# Test67 device checklist

Automated checks cannot substitute for these headset/phone checks. Test66 owner
acceptance covers Warthog/Ghost/tank, the Sniper grip, and improved ragdolls/parkour;
it does not mark the complete Test66 or earlier checklists done.

- Update each APK over Test66 without uninstalling; saves/settings retained.
- Holster both weapons: weapon crosshair disappears; withdraw either weapon:
  returns. Reticle-off still off. Shade/Ghost/tank reticles continue to aim.
  Tutorial light checks and scope alignment remain correct.
- AR/Pistol/Sniper/Shotgun/Rockets/Flamethrower/Needler: full/tactical/empty reload,
  left/right handed, mag pull/button eject/pouch/reinsert/drop/recover, insertion
  angle, charge/slide travel, feedback, cancellation and exact ammo conservation.
  Sniper must look and feel identical to accepted Test66.
- Button Sprint hold/toggle and Arm Run at 1/1.5/2/3x, stop/resume/menu/vehicle,
  PvP and nonauthorized co-op safety gates.
- Fists on all difficulties: one punch per stroke, release/rearm, quick switch
  among 3+ enemies, lunge/wall obstruction, block vs parry sounds/visual/haptics,
  enemies survive meaningful combos, shields/armor, deliberate rear takedown,
  normal firearms damage, elites counter, hunters heavy rules.
- Grab allies and enemies at chest/upper arm/leg from each side, both hands,
  lift/drag/slam/throw and recovery <=1s. No freehand stretching or anchor churn.
- Preserve curved-wall run, wall seams/grab/climb/launch, vehicle calibration and
  every immersive control, grenades, IK, holsters, collision/gunfire haptics.
- Campaign preparation from nothing/partial/already valid/corrupted files,
  folder import for both mods, original CE resource multi-select, missing list,
  repeated preparation, no duplicate downloads, install/switch/restore, cancellation
  and insufficient storage. Original maps/checkpoints retained. Test both APKs.
- Launch/join/host ordinary PvP and co-op, voice, map sharing, menus/touch/controller,
  performance and full level transitions. Wire/audio paths intentionally unchanged.

Report build, device/OS, active campaign/map, settings, actions and the launch log.
No acceptance claims until the owner provides the corresponding results.
