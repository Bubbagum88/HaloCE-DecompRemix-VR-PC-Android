# Test67 device checklist

Automated checks cannot substitute for these headset/phone checks. Test66 owner
acceptance covers Warthog/Ghost/tank, the Sniper grip, and improved ragdolls/parkour;
it does not mark the complete Test66 or earlier checklists done.

- Update each APK over Test66 without uninstalling; saves/settings retained.
- Holster both weapons: weapon crosshair disappears; withdraw either weapon:
  returns. Reticle-off still off. Shade/Ghost/tank reticles continue to aim.
  Tutorial light checks and scope alignment remain correct.
- AR/Pistol/Sniper/Shotgun/Rockets/Flamethrower/Needler: full/tactical/empty reload,
  left/right handed, mag pull/button eject/pouch/reinsert/drop/recover, insertion
  angle, charge/slide travel, feedback, cancellation and exact ammo conservation.
  Sniper must look and feel identical to accepted Test66.
- Button Sprint hold/toggle and Arm Run at 1/1.5/2/3x, stop/resume/menu/vehicle,
  PvP and nonauthorized co-op safety gates.
- Fists on all difficulties: one punch per stroke, release/rearm, quick switch
  among 3+ enemies, lunge/wall obstruction, block vs parry sounds/visual/haptics,
  enemies survive meaningful combos, shields/armor, deliberate rear takedown,
  normal firearms damage, elites counter, hunters heavy rules.
- COMBAT MOVES: identify sweep/overhead/jab windups and native contact timing.
  Duck a sweep, sidestep an overhead/jab, then try mistimed and insufficient
  movement: only a valid dodge should open a brief follow-up. Repeat with nearby
  enemies so cues and openings stay with the correct target. Verify attack
  blend-in/out, no arm stretching, and normal damage outside the avoided swing.
- Arm control: acquire the actual arm, strike that held arm with the other fist,
  release and use the short opening. A chest/leg hold must not qualify. Compare
  Grunts, shielded/unshielded Elites, Jackal front/flank and Hunter heavy rules.
  Check pinned feet and smooth kneeling where supported; native pose is expected
  for unsupported chains or while NPC/Jolt ownership is active. Kneeling is
  presentation only; collision remains native. Try jump/downward-hit pummel,
  missed hits, repeated contacts and recovery without player attachment/teleport.
- DEFLECT SHOTS: strike a moving projectile or airborne grenade with either
  closed empty fist (Grip + Trigger), then with a deliberate held-gun stroke.
  Ordinary gun aiming/holding and a stationary raised hand must not deflect.
  Check gun parry against melee too; reload/second-hand gun grips and other
  reserved hands must keep their own interactions. Repeat in left-hand mode.
  Returned shots should follow the stroke, with no target attraction or repeated
  deflection of the same projectile. Check wall obstruction, misses, fast shots,
  moving grenades and unchanged fuse/detonation; attached/stopped/settled grenades
  must retain native behavior. Hitscan attacks have no projectile to deflect.
- Toggle FIST COMBAT, COMBAT MOVES, DEFLECT SHOTS and HIT HIGHLIGHTS OFF/ON;
  verify each option's effect and persistence. With all combat options OFF,
  native combat remains. Test PvP and co-op clients: these additions must be OFF
  there. Test compatible co-op host with host actions disabled, then explicitly
  enabled; joining an incompatible peer must not leave local authority active.
- Grab allies and enemies at chest/upper arm/leg from each side, both hands,
  lift/drag/slam/throw and recovery <=1s. No freehand stretching or anchor churn.
- Preserve curved-wall run, wall seams/grab/climb/launch, vehicle calibration and
  every immersive control, grenades, IK, holsters, collision/gunfire haptics.
- Campaign preparation from nothing/partial/already valid/corrupted files,
  folder import for both mods, original CE resource multi-select, missing list,
  repeated preparation, no duplicate downloads, install/switch/restore, cancellation
  and insufficient storage. Original maps/checkpoints retained. Test both APKs.
- Altheros: select the original 10.24.2019 content and its required resource
  aliases; load b40 first to reproduce the reported blue-view failure case, then
  a10/a30/a50/b30/c10/c20/c40/d20/f10. Confirm actual world rendering and audio,
  first conversion and cached reload, checkpoint/save/reload, death/restart and
  BSP/level transitions. Retain saves while switching Altheros -> base -> SPV1
  -> Altheros. Watch Quest memory/loading behavior and collect a log on failure.
  Offline map/schema checks do not establish these runtime results.
- Launch/join/host ordinary PvP and co-op, voice, map sharing, menus/touch/controller,
  performance and full level transitions. Wire/audio paths intentionally unchanged.
  Confirm voice before and after campaign switches; no voice setting migration
  or changed voice behavior is intended.
- Lifecycle: interrupt combat/grab/parry with menu/pause, tracking loss/reacquire,
  recenter, death/respawn, vehicle entry/exit, map transition and host/client role
  changes. No stale strikes, cues, stun, owned hand or pose should survive; normal
  reload, grabs and haptics must resume. Measure busy-fight frame time on Quest.

Report build, device/OS, active campaign/map, settings, actions and the launch log.
No acceptance claims until the owner provides the corresponding results.
