# Test69 device checklist

Automated checks cannot substitute for these. Test68's and Test67's device
checklists still apply in full (TEST68-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md,
TEST67-DEVICE-CHECKLIST.md).

- Update each APK over Test68 or Test67 without uninstalling; saves and
  settings kept.
- The Altheros Experience: b40 (the blue screen) loads and plays. Then a10,
  a30 and a50 at least, and the others if time allows.
- VISR (night vision) toggles without errors; no tint or bloom is expected.
- a30: the gravity lift lifts you.
- No frame-rate counter appears in a10 or a30.
- SPV1 and the stock campaign still load (the script changes apply to every
  Custom Edition map).
- If a level still stops, send the launch log (it names the step).

Report build, device/OS, map, settings, actions and the launch log.
